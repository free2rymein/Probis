# Probis 2.0 Roadmap

Probis 2.0 begins as a clean prediction-market explorer. Intelligence features
return progressively after the discovery and charting experience is stable.

## Phase UI-1: Prediction Market Explorer

Build the responsive market browsing foundation: venue navigation, category
filters, market cards or rows, outcome probabilities, and clean empty states.

## Phase UI-2: Market Detail & Charts

Add market detail pages with normalized metadata, outcome state, resolution
context, and snapshot-driven probability, volume, liquidity, and open-interest
charts.

## Phase UI-3: Discovery & Search

Add search, sorting, category exploration, venue filtering, and lightweight
discovery surfaces for active and upcoming markets.

## Phase UI-4: Signals Overlay

Reintroduce bounded signal overlays as contextual annotations on explorer and
market-detail surfaces. Keep the explorer useful when signals are absent.

## Phase UI-5: Wallet Intelligence

Restore wallet interpretation selectively: aliases, behavior summaries,
specialization, and relevant market-flow context without raw address-heavy UI.

## Phase UI-6: Narrative Intelligence

Add explainable theme and narrative context derived from existing market and
signal data. Keep correlation language careful and avoid a generic news feed.

## Phase UI-7: Regime Intelligence

Layer in bounded regime interpretation for attention, volatility, liquidity,
and cross-market narrative spread after the underlying explorer data is
reliable.
